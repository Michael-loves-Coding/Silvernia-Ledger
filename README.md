# Silvernia Ledger

**Proof of Concept (POC)**: Comprehensive Silver Palace character + motive compendium

*Designed / mocked out by Michael Hubel*

---

## Purpose

- Provide Silver Palace players with easy-to-access information for all current released and upcoming characters, alongside motives, team compositions, at-a-glance ratings, etc.
- Enable building teams on the website and suggest any changes / upgrade order / additions
- Provide at-a-glance tier list rankings for every character and meta team
- Look up your own profile or other players' profiles through public profile information
- Suggest character pulls, upgrade priority, material domains to focus on, etc.
- Public display of character builds through UID or other front-facing information *(TBV)*
- Allow comparison of player builds to each other or to leaderboards *(TBV)*
- Background music made up of official Elementa games OST, with credit given (must be toggleable, as this could be annoying)
- Image / video carousels on the main page and character pages
- Videos from community TCs (theorycrafters) and content creators, with credit given and links to Discord channels, YouTube channels, social media, etc.
- Forum or page that lets community members upload teams / builds to share with others
- Epiphanies / gear page alongside recommended setups
- Possible map screen with a full scalable, responsive, interactive image of the map, where community members can mark resource locations, place pins with puzzle solutions, and much more
